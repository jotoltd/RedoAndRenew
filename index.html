Our Story

Why replace when
we can renew?

At Redo & Renew, we believe every piece of furniture has a story worth saving. We rescue forgotten, worn-out pieces from our local community and give them a fresh, stylish second life — keeping them out of landfills and in loving homes.

Charline transforms tired furniture into beautiful, bespoke pieces made to last. Because solid quality should never be swapped for flat-pack.

  • Hand-picked pieces rescued locally
  • Bespoke colour & finish, your way
  • Built to last, not to be replaced
